Graham Cheese Factory Marketplace

Role
Design · Modeling
Scope
Adaptive Reuse · Public Marketplace
Tools
Rhino 8 · Twinmotion
Located in Elnora, Indiana, this project turns an abandoned cheese factory into a flexible marketplace for local businesses and the community.
The design gives the building a new purpose while creating a place for vendors, events, and everyday activity as part of Elnora’s revitalization.
01Problem
An abandoned cheese factory at a rural crossroads in Elnora needed a new use that could support local entrepreneurs and give people a place to gather.
02Solution
The design keeps the existing industrial shell. Freestanding vendor stalls fill the main floor, while a mezzanine with long shared tables creates space for eating, working, and community events. A corrugated metal and glass exterior, along with a covered porch, gives the building a stronger presence at the edge of town.
03Research
Process

My initial instinct was to give the factory an industrial feel. But with the buildings existing sheet metal facade, we decided to keep that and transform this building into a strong statement about Elnora: "We embrace the future".
