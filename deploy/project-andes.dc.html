A custom NASCAR style paint scheme for the Andes #6 NextGen Toyota Camry, made for the iRacing sim racing series.

01Problem
Take the familiar Andes Crème de Menthe look, including the mint green mountains and script logo, and make it work on a 200 mph NextGen Camry without losing what makes the candy wrapper recognizable. 
02Solution
The paint scheme is built around the layered mountain graphic and deep Andes green. The goal was to create a wrap that feels consistent with modern schemes in todays NASCAR.
03PRECEDENT
Process

I took inspirations from bits and pieces of the above schemes to create a scheme that fits Andes brand.
