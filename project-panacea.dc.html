Panacea Junkiez

Client
Panacea Junkiez, Kinston, NC
Scope
Logo Refresh · Color · Type · Brand Guide
Year
2026
An organic juice and smoothie bar in Kinston, North Carolina. Real character and a good product, held back by a dated, inconsistent look.
01Problem

The name carries its own tension. “Panacea” reads wellness, “Junkiez” reads scrappy. The rebrand had to let each word keep its voice inside one polished, playful mark.

02Before

The original mark — hand-assembled from syringes, pills, and a stethoscope. Energetic, but clashing with the wellness positioning and unusable at small sizes.
