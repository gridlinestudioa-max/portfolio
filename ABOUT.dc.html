About

Based in Chicago.

I’m Will Potter, and this is where my work lives. I’m from Indiana and now work from Chicago, partnering remotely with small businesses and individuals wherever they are. My background spans branding, visualization and spatial design.

My goal isn’t to turn every business into a corporate brand. It’s to build an identity that reflects what makes it unique and helps it stand out.
