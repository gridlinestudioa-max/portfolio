How pricing works
$30 / hour

Same rate for every service. No packages, no per-item price list. You pay for the hours the work actually takes, and you see every one of them.

Example
An 8-hour logo project
Quote8 hours × $30 = $240 Final pricebetween $192 and $288 Deposit$120 at signing

If it starts heading past $288, the work pauses until you approve a revised estimate in writing.

01

Written quote

Before any work starts you get a written quote: the hours I estimate the project will take, × $30.

02

The 20% promise

The final price lands within 20% of the quote. If the work is on track to pass 120% of it, I stop and get your written approval of a revised estimate before going further.

03

Every hour logged

I log every hour I work on your project. The final invoice includes the itemized hours log, so you see exactly what you paid for.

04

Payment

50% deposit when you sign. The balance is due within 15 days of the final invoice.
